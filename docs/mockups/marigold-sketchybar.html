Marigold SketchyBar: prototype for #165

Throwaway. The M1 MacBook Air at default scaling (1440×900 points, no notch), with the macOS menu bar auto-hidden. The variant switcher at the bottom loads a starting preset; every control on the right changes it from there. Hover the top edge of the screen to reveal the menu bar (and an auto-hidden bar). Click items to try their click action. F or the button shows the screen at 1:1; in Chrome full screen (⌃⌘F) that's the real size on this Mac.

Built-in display: 1440×900
Second display: 1920×1080, to the right of the built-in one
